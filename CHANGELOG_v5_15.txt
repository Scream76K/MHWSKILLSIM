MH Wilds Skill Simulator v5.15

- Skill evaluation UI now clearly distinguishes real DPS calculation from priority-only evaluation.
- Modeled skills only receive a real Lv+1 DPS delta; unmodeled skill effects are not presented as fake DPS values.
- Critical Boost evaluation uses the current build's actual Critical Boost level rather than the Step 5 manual field.
- Displays baseline DPS and evaluation method in the skill evaluation table.
- Keeps the user-as-final-decision design: evaluation assists candidate exploration and does not auto-confirm a build.
